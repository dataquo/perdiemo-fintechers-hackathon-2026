# Required Documents Checklist

> Extracted from Tapaya KYB training pack: `Required-Documents-Checklist.pdf`

---

### Page 1

KYC/KYB documents List for Partners 
 
1. Partner Application Form 
2. PSP/ISO Agreement 
3. UBO declaration form 
4. Current extract from Official Commercial Register for the company status with visible 
owners and executives/directors* 
5. Current extract from Official Commercial Register for the parent companies with 
visible owners and executives/directors* 
6. Articles of Association last amendment/Memorandum of Association/ Certification of 
Incorporation* 
7. List of Shareholders (if no information about UBOs is available on the extract from the 
Commercial Register)* 
8. Passport/ID of the Owner(s)  
9. Passport/ID and Recent Utility Bill (proof of address) of the Director(s) - not older than 
3 months  
10. Bank Letter, containing IBAN for remittances , with company name and address - not 
older than 3 months 
11. License (if the Company operates as a regulated entity) 
12. PCI DSS documentation 
13. Latest Financial/audit statements 
14. Internal Policies 
 
Will not be requested from the Merchant if Fintechers Pay verifies the details in the official Trade Register.
