## Chapter 1 - Introductory provisions

### Article 1 - Purpose and scope

1. This internal regulation sets out the policy on the use of artificial intelligence (AI) and the rules for its use within the Company.
2. This regulation applies to all Employees and Members of elected bodies.

### Article 2 - Definitions

See *(cross-reference left blank in the original)*.

## Chapter 2 - Strategy for the use of artificial intelligence

1. The Company intends to take full advantage of artificial intelligence to support and improve the efficiency of the services it provides and of its supporting processes.
2. The Company is fully aware of its responsibility for its activities, the services it provides and regulatory compliance, including where AI is used. It does not waive this responsibility in any way.
3. The Company intends to use AI where it is economically advantageous, makes the provision of its services easier, and the use of AI does not represent an unacceptable risk (exceeding the risk appetite).
4. Where economically beneficial for the Company and in line with its risk appetite, the Company uses solutions shared with other members of the Partners financial group.
5. The Company will allocate sufficient capacity to managing the risks associated with the use of AI, in particular for selecting AI solutions, assessing and managing their risks, and controls.
6. When using AI suppliers, the Company will be fully transparent towards clients and towards the supervisory and oversight authorities.

## Chapter 3 - Use of artificial intelligence in the Company

### Article 3 - Roles and responsibilities

1. The AI Sponsor is responsible for the use of AI to support a specific process, function or activity, and for managing the related risks within the meaning of regulation *(reference left blank in the original)*.
2. The AI Sponsor may designate a Senior Employee to perform the AI Sponsor's duties under this internal regulation.

### Article 3 - Use of AI from a third party

*(Numbered "Article 3" a second time in the original.)*

1. Where there is an intention to use an AI tool or service operated or supported by a third party (supplier), the Outsourcing Sponsor or a Senior Employee designated by them proceeds in accordance with regulation *(reference left blank in the original)*.
2. When assessing risks and when controlling and reviewing the use of AI from a third party, the specific features of AI are taken into account, in particular the rapid development of this technology. In particular, the AI Sponsor or a Senior Employee designated by them shortens the interval of the regular review of the service, taking into account the risks identified.

### Article 4 - Internal use of AI

1. Internal use of AI means the implementation of an AI model as a computer program that runs internally in the Company's data centre and is isolated from the manufacturer's infrastructure. In connection with such an implementation of an AI model, no training data, statistics, analytics or other information is received or sent between the Company and any server outside the Company.
2. Where AI is intended for internal use, with no third party supporting the operation or functioning of the service, the AI Sponsor ensures that an AI use proposal is prepared and its risks are assessed.
3. The AI use proposal contains in particular the following items:
    1. Description of the internal AI (reference to documentation)
    2. Processes or activities in which the AI will be used, including a specific description of how and to what extent it will be used
    3. Benefits of introducing the AI and success factors
    4. Costs and a cost-benefit analysis
    5. Main risks identified and a reference to the risk analysis carried out under *(reference left blank in the original)*
    6. Rules for monitoring and controlling the AI
    7. Rules for training the AI model (data sources, ensuring the security of training data)
4. The Head of the Operational Risk Management department keeps a register of the AI tools in use (external AI tools may be part of the register of outsourcing arrangements or ICT suppliers under regulation *(reference left blank in the original)*).

### Article 5 - AI-specific risks

1. When identifying and assessing risks in connection with an external or internal risk assessment, the responsible employees also assess risks associated with:
    1. Possible bias (AI bias) of the AI tool
    2. Insufficient control over the AI's procedure or outputs
    3. Discrimination against the persons concerned
    4. Insufficient documentability of procedures
    5. Breach of legal regulations, in particular in the areas of copyright, consumer protection, personal data protection and the regulation of the use of AI (the Artificial Intelligence Act)
    6. Disclosure of otherwise inaccessible (confidential or raw training) data to an unauthorised person in the form of AI output (e.g. in a chat with the AI)
    7. Breach of the isolation of the AI model, i.e. unauthorised sharing of training data or already processed data outside the Company.
